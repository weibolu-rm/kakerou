> [!bug] Grammar errors
> 1. ✗ 単なるの気の迷いか → ✓ 単なる気の迷いなのか
>    単なる is a な-adjective: it attaches directly to the noun — no の.
>    (3rd time · recurring — [[mistake-log#な-adjective + noun attachment]])
>
> 2. ✗ 本当の興味か → ✓ 本当の興味なのか
>    A nominal embedded question needs なのか, not bare か.
>
> 3. ✗ 頑張ろうと思います → ✓ 頑張ります
>    Plain volitional inside a polite diary — the closing line slipped registers.
>    (5th time · recurring — [[mistake-log#Register mixing (ですます vs plain form)]])

> [!warning] Misleading vocabulary & phrasing
